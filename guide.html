01

Put it on your phone

Open dosenft.github.io/dwk-vault once, then add it to your home screen — it opens full-screen like a real app, and the lock-screen controls work.

  • iPhone / iPad — must be Safari. Tap Share (square with an arrow), scroll down, Add to Home Screen.
  • Android — Chrome, three dots, Add to Home screen.
  • Laptop — look at the right end of the address bar for a small screen-with-an-arrow icon and click Install.

It works without signal too — everything but the recordings themselves.

02

Press play anywhere

Every page leads with one round play button. Press it and you land in the Reel — the listening room — already playing.

Sessions with a recording are marked with three small bars beside their title. Five of them have audio so far.

03

The Reel

Every moment of the night is a card. Scroll them with your thumb — the room changes colour as you move and the recording follows you. Stop scrolling and it settles there.

  • −15 / +15 nudge the recording fifteen seconds.
  • The arrows either side jump a whole moment.
  • The coloured ticks on the bar are the moments, placed where they really happened. Colour is what kind of moment it was.
  • ✕ at the top left closes it. So does the back gesture.
04

Every session is a picture before it's a wall of text

Under the play button on each session there's a strip of coloured ticks — one per moment, spaced by when it happened. You can see a night open in conversation, turn gold with lore and end in a blood-dark run before you read a word.

Touch a tick and it tells you what that moment was. Touch it again and it takes you there.

05

Everything you change, everyone sees

This is the part worth understanding. There is one vault, not five copies. When you fix a spelling or add a picture, it appears for the whole table.

The first time you change anything it asks your name. That's so the record shows who did what — nothing more. Answer it once and it never asks again.

06

Turning on Edit

✎ Edit lives in the bar at the top of every page. Tap it and a green strip appears along the bottom. That strip is how you know you're editing.

While it's on, anything you can change is underlined. Tap the underline and type.

Tap ✎ Editing again when you're done. You don't need to save — it saves itself.

07

Your own character

Open Driftwood Kin and tap your portrait. In edit mode you get:

  • Add a picture — the big image behind your name. Any photo or art; it's resized for you.
  • The line under your name — your class, title, whatever the table would say about you.
  • One line in your own words — the only writing in the vault that isn't the recording's.
  • Add to the gallery — more images, shown as a strip.

Under that is their arc: one line per session you were in, in order. Tap a night to open it.

08

A name spelled wrong

The recordings mis-hear names constantly. One place said Caramel, Caremall and Care Mall for the same town.

In edit mode, tap Fix a spelling everywhere in the green strip. Type what's wrong and what it should be. It shows you how many places it found and what they look like before you commit.

It changes every session, moment, thread and codex entry at once.

09

Tidying the Codex

The Codex is everyone and everything the recordings mentioned. It's messy by nature. In edit mode each card gets four small controls:

  • rename — correct the name; every session follows.
  • merge… — two cards that are the same thing become one, and nothing is lost.
  • the dropdown — move it to the right section (a god that's really an Asterum, say).
  • hide — remove a card that was never real. The session notes keep it.
10

You can't break it

Genuinely. Every change is written as a new line in a list — nothing is ever overwritten and the page is physically unable to delete anything already there.

So the worst you can do is make a mess, and a mess can be wound back. If something goes wrong, say so and it gets undone. Don't be careful at the expense of being useful.

Something wrong, or something missing? Tell Marcus. Nothing you do in here is permanent damage — every change is logged and can be wound back.

If a recording won't play on your device, open the playback check, press the button, and send him the result.
